# Hostel Complaint & Maintenance Management System

## 1. Problem Statement

Hostel students may face problems such as electrical faults,
plumbing issues, cleaning problems, and damaged furniture.

When complaints are handled manually, it can become difficult to
track their status and find previous complaints.

This project provides a simple digital system for registering,
searching, tracking, and managing hostel maintenance complaints.

## 2. Objectives

- Register hostel complaints digitally.
- Store complaint details in an organized way.
- Search complaints easily.
- Track complaint status.
- Display basic complaint statistics.
- Reduce difficulty in managing hostel maintenance complaints.

## 3. Target Users

- Hostel students
- Hostel maintenance staff

## 4. Scope

The system focuses on recording and managing hostel maintenance
complaints.

It includes complaint registration, viewing, searching, status
updates, and statistics.

The current system does not include online payment, real-time
staff tracking, or external notification services.

## 5. Main Functional Features

1. Complaint Registration
2. Complaint Viewing
3. Complaint Search
4. Complaint Status Management
5. Complaint Statistics
